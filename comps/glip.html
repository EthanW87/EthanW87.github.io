Venture & Showcase Overview

The General's Lab Innovation Program showcases hardware ventures and engineering solutions evaluated by panels of industry leaders. We pitched the business viability, Bill of Materials (BOM) cost breakdown, and technical architecture of our TriPAD site monitoring unit, securing 3rd Place Overall.

  • Hardware BOM Optimization: Validated sensor component pricing and manufacturing feasibility for field-ready deployments.
