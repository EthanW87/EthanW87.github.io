System Overview

A high-complexity mechatronics personal project aimed at designing a fully transforming robot platform heavily inspired by Optimus Prime. Modeled entirely in Fusion 360, the mechanical design integrates over 30 degrees of freedom, featuring intricate linkage systems, custom structural housings for battery management, and high-torque servo linkages for reliable vehicle-to-biped reconfiguration.

Servo Kinematics & Sequential Actuation

Bench testing individual servo channels (0 to 10) sequentially to verify PWM control signal isolation, power distribution under load, and linkage range-of-motion prior to full assembly integration.

Sequential 0–10 servo sweep sequence verifying PWM timing and physical joint limits.

Embedded Electronics & Circuitry

Complete wiring harness integration featuring multi-channel servo drivers, voltage regulation, power rails, and microcontroller communication layout.

Wired electronics test bench with microcontroller, servo driver breakout, and power management setup.
