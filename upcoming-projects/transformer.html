Transformer Mechanical CAD

Custom 3D-printed assembly designed in Fusion 360 to house the internal battery, motor drivers, and servo actuators. Click and drag to orbit the 3D model.

System Overview

A high-complexity mechatronics personal project aimed at designing a fully transforming robot platform heavily inspired by Optimus Prime. Modeled entirely in Fusion 360, the mechanical design integrates over 30 degrees of freedom, featuring intricate linkage systems, custom structural housings for battery management, and high-torque servo linkages for reliable vehicle-to-biped reconfiguration.
