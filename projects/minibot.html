Full arena run demonstrating navigation, wall-triggered 360 spin, reverse maneuver, and scoop actuation.

The First-Year Odyssey

Every robotics journey starts somewhere! As one of my earliest university engineering challenges, this project was all about getting hands-on with real-time feedback loops, sensor integration, and mechanical actuation under physical arena constraints.

The task was simple in theory, but chaotic in practice: build a mobile platform capable of navigating an unknown track, handling intersection decisions, interacting with physical arena walls, and carrying out a full payload pickup-and-delivery cycle without human intervention.

The Mission Sequence

1. Line Tracking

Reads floor reflectivity via IR sensor arrays to adjust differential motor speeds and turn tightly at track crossroad intersections.

2. Wall Detection & Spin

Ultrasonic range sensors detect wall obstacles, triggering an immediate 360° victory spin and precise reverse alignment.

3. Scoop & Transport

Actuates a motorized scoop mechanism to secure the target load, re-engaging line tracking toward the unloading zone.

4. Drop-off Sequence

Identifies the designated crossroad goal, reverses servo angles to deposit the payload, and completes the autonomous cycle.

Hardware Breakdown

  • Ground Guidance: Multi-channel IR Reflectance Sensors (measuring differential light reflection between floor surface and black tape).
  • Obstacle Sensing: Ultrasonic Distance Sensors for continuous proximity detection along the vehicle heading.
  • Actuation: Micro servo motors for the custom 3D-printed payload scoop and high-torque DC motor drive.
